Delete your account

In the Youemi app

  1. Open Youemi and sign in to your account.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm permanent deletion.

What is deleted

Deletion permanently removes your account, profile, address, location, photo, and personal activity. If you are the only member of the couple space, that private space and its content are also deleted.

If a partner remains linked, content you already shared remains in their space, but the link to your profile is removed. This action cannot be undone.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app, contact allankleinpro@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.
