Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

About Youemi

Youemi, published by Allan KLEIN, lets couples create a shared private space.

Data we collect

We collect the account details you enter: display name, username, email address, and optional gender. Depending on how you use the app, we may also process your profile photo, notes, drawings, journey cards, status messages, saved places, and their photos.

When you choose to use location, we collect the address and precise coordinates needed to place locations on your couple map. Menstrual-cycle settings are collected only if you choose to enable that feature.

Use and sharing

This data is used only to provide, synchronise, and secure Youemi. It is not sold or used for advertising or tracking. Content you choose to share in the couple space is visible to the linked partner.

Youemi does not use contacts, microphone access, background location, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

Account data is stored while the account is active. From the Profile screen, account deletion permanently removes your account, profile, address, location, photo, and personal activity.

If you are the only member, the private couple space and its content are also deleted. If a partner remains linked, content already shared with that partner remains in their space, but the link to your profile is removed. Deletion is irreversible.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, remove your photo, change your address, or decline permissions at any time in your device settings. For any privacy request, contact allankleinpro@gmail.com.
