Frequently asked questions

How do I contact support?

Email allankleinpro@gmail.com.

How do I delete my account?

In Youemi, open Profile, then choose Delete account. You will need to enter your password. See Delete your account for details.

Why does Youemi request location?

Location is used only if you choose it, to place locations on your couple map. You can decline or change permission at any time in your device settings.

What can my partner see?

Content you choose to share in the couple space is visible to the partner linked to that space.
